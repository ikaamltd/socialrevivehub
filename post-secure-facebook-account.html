How to Secure Your Facebook Account After a Security Incident

Simple steps for reviewing sessions, strengthening login protection and regaining control.

Check your active sessions

Review the devices and sessions linked to your account. Sign out of locations you do not recognize and change the password using the official Facebook settings.

Protect the linked email

Make sure your email inbox is secure. Use a unique password, review recovery methods and enable two-factor authentication where offered.

Use official recovery

If you are locked out, start with Meta’s official account recovery process. Avoid sharing passwords, codes or recovery links with third parties.

Stay protected

Keep devices updated, check who has access to your Pages and Business Portfolio, and remain skeptical of urgent messages claiming to be support.
